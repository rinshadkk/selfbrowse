# Code of conduct

This project should be a good place to ask questions and contribute, whatever your experience or background.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback plainly and kindly.
- Respect other people's time and privacy.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexual attention or content.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is taking part in or representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem [privately on GitHub](https://github.com/rinshadkk/selfbrowse/security/advisories/new) and start the
title with "Conduct". Only the maintainer sees it. You can also use GitHub's
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) tools.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who do not follow this code, and will
explain why where it is safe to do so.
